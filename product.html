Will it fit around my sink?+

The current design measures 26 × 17.75 inches overall, with a 19 × 14.25 inch opening. Measure the sink base and clear countertop space. Fit also depends on sink shape and placement.

How do I wash it?+

Machine wash cold and hang or lay flat to dry. Do not use bleach or fabric softener.
